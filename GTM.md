# Nexus Go-to-Market Strategy & Launch Plan (GTM.md)

## 1. Executive Summary & Positioning
**Nexus** is the autonomous, commercial-grade community operating platform designed specifically for freelancer collectives, software academies, and digital agencies operating on Discord and the modern web.

### Positioning Statement
> *"For community owners who want to turn an active chat server into an economic powerhouse, Nexus provides the first complete, AI-driven operating system: from adaptive skill verification and talent matching to visual automations, escrow milestone tracking, and verifiable reputation."*

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments

| Segment | Pain Points | Primary Value Driver | Champion Persona |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Freelancer Communities & Guilds** | Member flakes, trust breakdown in deals, manual vetting overhead, unpaid middlemen | Escrow milestones, adaptive vetting, smart client matching | Community Founders, Senior Freelancers |
| **Coding & Design Academies / Bootcamps** | Scaled code reviews, portfolio evaluation, alumni tracking, certificate fraud | Code Lab auto-grader, Design Lab WCAG checks, Verifiable Credentials | Lead Instructors, Program Directors |
| **Distributed Digital Agencies** | Contractor discovery, scope creep, milestone disputes, cross-platform coordination | AI Project Manager, talent graph, contract templates, billing integration | Operations Directors, Agency Partners |
| **Creator & Technical Substack Hubs** | Free-to-paid conversion, event management, sponsor management, churn | Paywalled perks, hackathon platform, sponsor portal, weekly executive brief | Content Creators, Community Managers |

- **Commission**: 20% recurring revenue share for 12 months for every community onboarded onto Pro or Business plans.

- **Incentive for New Tenants**: Invited servers receive \$25 in AI inference credits upon bot activation.

## 5. Community Template Gallery Strategy
Pre-configured templates allow community owners to provision a complete ecosystem in under 60 seconds via `/setup`:
1. **Freelancer Dev Hub**: `#jobs`, `#escrow-deals`, `#code-reviews`, 3-tier vetting, smart client matching.
2. **Design Collective**: `#critique`, `#accessibility-audit`, blind voting challenges, portfolio generator.
3. **Coding Bootcamp**: Modular course tracks, adaptive quizzes, sandboxed challenges, verifiable graduation diplomas.
4. **Agency Syndicate**: Private client portals, scope creep monitoring, milestone escrow, team assembly.
